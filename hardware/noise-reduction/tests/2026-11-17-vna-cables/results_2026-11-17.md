# Results: insertion loss and return loss at 160 MHz

Computed 2026-11-17 from the S2P files in this folder (|S21| and |S11| at the 160.0 MHz sample;
the files hold 101 points at 1.2 MHz spacing, so no interpolation was needed).

| Component | Insertion loss, as read | Return loss | VSWR | Loss over 140–180 MHz | Group delay |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thru (calibration check) | 0.00 dB (S21 +0.006 dB) | 33.4 dB | 1.04 | −0.01 to 0.00 dB | 0.0 ns |
| 15 ft RG-316 extension | 1.27 dB | 44.2 dB | 1.01 | 1.21–1.40 dB | 20.0 ns |
| Common mode choke | 0.21 dB | 27.1 dB | 1.09 | 0.15–0.24 dB | 2.7 ns |
| Extension + choke in series | 1.64 dB | 26.6 dB | 1.10 | 1.52–1.74 dB | 24.2 ns |
| 88 cm RG-316 (test cable) | 0.21 dB | 26.4 dB | 1.10 | 0.16–0.25 dB | 2.8 ns |
| Choke + 88 cm RG-316 in series | 0.47 dB | 25.9 dB | 1.11 | 0.43–0.51 dB | 7.0 ns |

**The series reading is 0.17 dB above the sum of the parts (1.48 dB), and 1.5 ns longer.** Both
offsets are steady across 100–220 MHz (0.14 ± 0.05 dB, 1.5 ns), so they are not mismatch ripple;
the mismatch loss of every component is 0.01 dB or less. They match what calibrating with a 30 cm
thru cable does: the VNA treats that cable as lossless and zero-length, so every reading is the
component *minus* the thru cable. Two separate readings subtract it twice; the one series reading
subtracts it once. The difference is therefore the thru cable's own loss and delay, about 0.15 dB
and 1.5 ns.

**So each reading above understates the component's true loss by about 0.15 dB.** Corrected
estimates: extension about 1.4 dB, choke about 0.35 dB, the two in series about 1.8 dB. The
correction also makes the extension's electrical length agree with its physical length: 21.5 ns
over 4.57 m is a velocity factor of 0.71, close to RG-316's 0.695, against 0.76 uncorrected.

**The short-cable pair agrees the same way.** Choke plus 88 cm cable reads 0.47 dB against a sum
of 0.42 dB, again with 1.5 ns of extra delay: the same thru-cable signature. Corrected, the 88 cm
cable has a velocity factor of 0.69, matching RG-316.

**Path loss for the signal-generator test** (short cable plus choke): about **0.6 dB** at 160 MHz,
the 0.47 dB reading plus the 0.15 dB thru correction. The N-to-SMA adapter at the generator was not
measured and may add a few hundredths of a dB.

**Smaller points.**
- The VNA sweeps 401 points; NanoVNA-Saver exported 101. The traces are flat enough that this does
  not matter.
- The male-male SMA adapter for the 15 ft series measurement was missing from the CSV and has been
  added by Michael.
